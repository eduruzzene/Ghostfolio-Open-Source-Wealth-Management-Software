## 🎨 How to Use Ghostfolio

### Adding Your First Investment

1. **Click "Create Asset"** - Look for the green button labeled "Create Asset" on the main dashboard.
2. **Choose Asset Type** - Select whether you are adding a stock, ETF, or cryptocurrency.
3. **Search for Your Asset** - Type the name or ticker symbol (e.g., "AAPL" for Apple, "BTC" for Bitcoin). Ghostfolio will search its built-in database of thousands of assets.
4. **Select Your Asset** - Click on the correct match from the search results.
5. **Enter Quantity** - Type how much of this asset you own (e.g., 10 shares of Apple).
6. **Set Purchase Price** - Enter the price you paid per unit, or click "Auto-fill" to use the current market price.
7. **Click "Save"** - Your asset is now tracked!

### Understanding Your Dashboard

- **Total Value** - The top number shows the combined value of all your investments in your chosen currency.
- **Performance** - The green or red percentage shows how your portfolio has changed since you bought your assets.
- **Charts** - The line graph shows historical performance. The pie chart shows how your investments are distributed across different categories.

### Backup Your Data

To keep your data safe, go to **Settings** (gear icon in the top right) → **Export Data**. This downloads a backup file you can store anywhere safe. To restore, go to **Settings** → **Import Data** and select your backup file.

## 🔍 Troubleshooting

### "The App Won't Start"
- Make sure your firewall is not blocking local connections. Allow Ghostfolio through your firewall when prompted.
- Check that no other program is using port 4200. If you have Skype or another service using this port, close it and try again.

### "Prices Are Not Updating"
- Ensure you have an active internet connection.
- Wait a few minutes and refresh the page - price APIs sometimes have delays.

### "I Forgot My Data"
- Ghostfolio does not have passwords or accounts, so you cannot be locked out. However, if you uninstall the software, your data is gone permanently. Always use the Export feature to back up regularly.

## 📦 Uninstalling Ghostfolio

If you decide to stop using Ghostfolio:
- **Windows**: Go to Settings → Apps → Find Ghostfolio → Uninstall
- **Mac**: Drag the Ghostfolio app to the Trash
- Your financial data will be deleted. Make sure to export first if you want to keep records.
